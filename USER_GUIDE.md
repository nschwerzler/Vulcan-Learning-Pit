# Vulcan Learning Pit - User Guide

## Welcome, Young Scholar 🖖

This guide will help you navigate the Vulcan Learning Pit and make the most of your training sessions.

## Getting Started

### 1. Launch the Application
- Run the application by executing `dotnet run` in the VulcanLearningPit directory
- Or open the solution in Visual Studio and press F5

### 2. Set Up Your Profile
On the startup screen, you'll need to:
- **Enter your name**: This identifies you on the leaderboard
- **Select your grade level**: Choose from grades 4-8
- Click **"START TRAINING SESSION"** to begin

## During a Training Session

### Understanding the Interface

#### Header Bar
- **🖖 Vulcan Learning Pit**: Application title
- **🏆 Leaderboard**: Click to view rankings
- **⭐ Tokens**: Your earned tokens (currency of achievement)
- **📊 Score**: Your total points

#### Mentor Section
- **Avatar**: 🖖 Vulcan salute representing your wise mentor
- **Message Box**: Spock-style guidance, encouragement, and wisdom

#### Problem Info Bar
- **Subject**: Current topic (Math, Logic, Reading, or Science)
- **Difficulty**: Current challenge level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert)
- **⏱ Timer**: Countdown in seconds - answer before it hits zero!
- **Switch Subject**: Change topics any time (great for ADD/ADHD support)

#### Problem Area
- **Passage** (Reading only): Text to read for comprehension questions
- **Question**: The problem to solve
- **Answer Options**: Multiple choice - select one by clicking the radio button
- **Feedback**: Shows results after submitting an answer

#### Action Buttons
- **SUBMIT ANSWER**: Check your answer (only works when an option is selected)
- **NEXT PROBLEM**: Move to the next challenge
- **END SESSION**: Finish your training session

## How to Earn Points & Tokens

### Base Rewards (by difficulty)
- **Easy**: 10 points, 1 token
- **Medium**: 20 points, 2 tokens
- **Hard**: 30 points, 3 tokens
- **Expert**: 50 points, 5 tokens

### Time Bonuses
Answer quickly for extra rewards:
- **≤50% of time**: +15 bonus points, +1 bonus token
- **≤75% of time**: +10 bonus points, +1 bonus token
- **≤100% of time**: +5 bonus points, +1 bonus token

Example: Solve an Expert problem (50 points) in under half the time limit = 65 points + 6 tokens!

## The Adaptive System

### How Difficulty Changes
The system automatically adjusts based on your performance:

**Difficulty Increases When:**
- You answer 3 problems correctly in a row
- Your success rate is above 80% with recent correct answers

**Difficulty Decreases When:**
- You answer 3 problems incorrectly in a row
- Your success rate falls below 50%

### Subject Selection
The system intelligently chooses subjects:
- **70% of the time**: Focuses on your weakest subject (where you need practice)
- **30% of the time**: Random variety (keeps things interesting, helps with ADD)

## Tips for Success

### 1. Read Carefully
Take time to understand the question before selecting an answer. The timer is generous.

### 2. Use Subject Switching
If you're feeling stuck or frustrated with one topic, click "Switch Subject" to:
- Keep your mind fresh
- Maintain motivation
- Prevent burnout

### 3. Learn From Mistakes
After an incorrect answer, read the explanation. The mentor will provide encouragement and the system shows the correct answer.

### 4. Watch for Patterns
As you progress:
- Problems get harder when you're doing well
- Problems get easier if you're struggling
- The system targets your weak areas

### 5. Speed vs. Accuracy
Both matter! Fast answers earn bonuses, but wrong answers hurt your progress. Find your balance.

## The Leaderboard

### Viewing Rankings
Click the **🏆 Leaderboard** button in the header to see:
- Current rankings
- Student names
- Grade levels
- Total scores and tokens

### How Rankings Work
Students are ranked by:
1. **Total Score** (primary)
2. **Total Tokens** (tiebreaker)

Compete with honor and strive for excellence!

## Session Management

### Pausing
There's no pause button - the Vulcan way is to stay focused. If you need a break:
- Finish the current problem
- Click "END SESSION"
- Start a new session when ready

### Ending a Session
Click **"END SESSION"** when you want to stop:
- Your progress is saved to your profile
- You'll see a summary message
- Your tokens and score remain for the leaderboard

### Multiple Sessions
Each time you start a new session:
- Your total score and tokens carry over
- New problems are generated
- Difficulty continues from where you left off
- Statistics accumulate over all sessions

## Troubleshooting

### Timer Runs Out
If the timer hits zero:
- The problem is marked as incomplete
- You'll see a time's up message
- Click "NEXT PROBLEM" to continue
- The system may adjust difficulty down

### Can't Select an Answer
- Make sure you click directly on the radio button or text
- Only one answer can be selected at a time
- Selection clears when moving to the next problem

### Switch Subject Button
Use this liberally:
- No penalty for switching
- Helps maintain engagement
- Supports varied learning styles
- Especially helpful for ADD/ADHD
